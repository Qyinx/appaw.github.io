# PSA advisor content playbook (off-page)

Trust for high-value card intake is built by showing the process in public, not by claiming a storefront you do not operate. Use this list when publishing on Instagram, 小紅書, or short video.

## Positioning line

Appaw is a high-value grading advisor who coordinates PSA submission at 138 Arena. We help collectors decide whether submission is worth the fee and wait, then track the batch online.

## Topic backlog (repeatable formats)

1. **Tool check** — microscope / loupe / angled light / centering overlay on a real card; narrate what would stop a realistic PSA 10 path.
2. **When not to submit** — crease, dent, heavy whitening; show the fee and time cost avoided.
3. **Packing for transit** — sleeve and shock materials before the batch leaves Hong Kong.
4. **BAT tracking** — phone + reference lookup on `/business/psa-grading/track/` (show UI, not customer PII).
5. **Tier choice** — when a strong card may justify a faster PSA tier (illustrative advice, not a score guarantee).
6. **After PSA 10** — cert check, outer protector, optional consign via `/business/card-trading/`.

## Copy rules

- Follow [`guides-content.md`](guides-content.md): 書面語 on zh captions; 提交鑑定 / 置中; no 送評 in body.
- Do not publish absolute hit-rate claims (e.g. “95% PSA 10”) without measured data.
- Attribute venue vs Appaw correctly (138 Arena = drop-off location; Appaw = submission operations).
